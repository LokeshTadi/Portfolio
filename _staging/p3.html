01

Zoho Catalyst Queuing & Bussing System

Event-driven backend · Billing automation

Zoho

The problem

Work requests arrived in bursts and had to be processed reliably, in order, and without losing any item. Each processed request also needed an invoice and a subscription created, which was previously manual, slow and error-prone.

My approach

I built an event-driven queue on Zoho Catalyst. Incoming tasks are accepted through the API Gateway, buffered and dispatched with Signals, and processed by a long-running service on AppSail. Business logic lives in Catalyst Functions, and a bussing layer routes messages between services so each component stays independent and scalable.

Billing automation

When a queue item finishes, the system calls the Zoho Billing and Zoho Subscriptions APIs to create the invoice and subscription automatically, closing the loop from task to payment with no human step.

Architecture

Client / API Gateway→Signals (events)→AppSail worker→Catalyst Functions→Zoho Billing + Subscriptions

Outcome

  • Real-time task processing that scales with load.
  • Invoicing and subscriptions created automatically for every processed queue.
  • Part of the automation work that cut manual effort by 40%.

02

ERP Customisation: Lead-to-Invoice

Zoho Creator · CRM · Subscriptions · Books

Zoho

The problem

Sales, finance and operations lived in separate tools. A won deal meant re-keying data into billing and accounting, and managers had no single view of a customer from first lead to paid invoice.

My approach

I customised ERP modules (Inventory, Finance, HR) in Zoho Creator and Zoho CRM, then connected them to Zoho Subscriptions and Zoho Books. Blueprints enforce the sales process stage by stage, while Deluge functions pass data between systems at the right moment.

How it flows

Lead (CRM)→Blueprint approval→Creator ERP module→Subscription→Invoice in Books

Outcome

  • Seamless lead-to-invoice and financial workflows with no duplicate entry.
  • CRM modules, layouts and workflows tuned for a 35% efficiency gain in sales and lead management.
  • Role-based dashboards and validation using Deluge and Creator permission sets.

03

Zoho Creator + CRM Lead & Approval Suite

Lead management · Approvals · Email automation

Zoho

The problem

Leads were followed up inconsistently, approvals travelled by email and chat, and nobody could tell where a request was stuck.

My approach

I developed several Zoho Creator applications integrated with Zoho CRM. Leads sync between both systems, approval stages are modelled as explicit workflows with the right approvers, and automated email workflows notify people at each step. Serverless Catalyst functions handle integrations with Books and external applications.

Outcome

  • Clear, auditable approval trail for every request.
  • Faster lead response through automatic notifications.
  • One consistent data model across CRM, Creator and Books.
